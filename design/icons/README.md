# Icon concepts

Five concepts generated by `tool/generate_icon_concepts.py`, in the theme's palette (asphalt, chalk, orange, teal), with no existing logo or brand. For each concept: `N_name.png` (1024×1024), `N_name_48.png` and `N_name_48_grayscale.png` (legibility test at small size and in monochrome), `N_name_on_light.png` (rendered on a light background). Overview: `icon_concepts_contact_sheet.png`.

1. **`1_front_hoop`** — hoop seen from the front (backboard + rim + net). At 48 px the orange rim blends into the white backboard: the "hoop" identity disappears.
2. **`2_stylized_net`** — net stylized as diamonds. A strong silhouette at large size, but the weave detail turns to mush at 48 px.
3. **`3_pin_ball`** — a map marker fused with a ball (seams inside the marker's head). **Chosen**: the only concept that stays legible at 48 px and in grayscale, and the only one that shows both what the app does (locate) and its subject (basketball).
4. **`4_abstract_geometric`** — a ball bouncing off the corner of a tile, teal accent. Clean, but doesn't evoke basketball or location without the color context.
5. **`5_top_down_hoop`** — a rim seen from above, net woven from rope at the center. Very strong at large size; in grayscale at 48 px the weave disappears and only a generic ring remains.

To change the icon: reuse the body of `concept_pin_ball()` (or another function) from `tool/generate_icon_concepts.py` in `tool/generate_brand_assets.py`, rerun the latter, then `dart run flutter_launcher_icons` and `dart run flutter_native_splash:create`.
